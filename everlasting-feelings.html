04 / POETRY GAME · BEng THESIS · 2021

Everlasting Feelings

A Unity-based adaptation of Feihualing that turns the exchange of Chinese poetic verses into a timed challenge.

Chinese poetryUnityAI-driven play
Context
Individual BEng thesis · Beijing Language and Culture University
Practice
Cultural research, game development and playtesting
Tools & methods
Unity · AI-driven poetry battle mechanics

The question

How can a traditional poetic game become an approachable digital encounter?

Players choose a theme and respond with verses within a time limit. The exchange continues until the computer cannot answer. The project uses a familiar cultural activity as the basis for a digital game.

01 / The approach

A tradition as the interaction

The core mechanic is based on Feihualing, a Chinese poetic game. Rather than using poetry only as background decoration, the project makes the exchange of verses the action that drives play.

02 / The approach

An earlier step in the same direction

This BEng thesis was supervised by Prof. Yingbin Liu. The CV documents cultural research and qualitative playtesting around engagement, usability and cultural learning. It is retained as a compact earlier project connecting culture and computation.
